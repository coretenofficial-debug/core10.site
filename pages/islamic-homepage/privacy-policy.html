Your Privacy
Matters

Last updated: May 2026 · Islamic Homepage v1.02

We respect your privacy — absolutely

Islamic Homepage is built with respect for your privacy at its core. This policy explains what data we collect, how it's used, and your rights — in plain language.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect the minimum data necessary to provide you with a great experience. All data remains in your browser unless you explicitly choose to share it.

  • Location Data: We request access to your device's geolocation solely to calculate accurate prayer times for your local area. This data is not transmitted to our servers — it remains in your browser's local storage.
  • Usage Preferences: Your settings (clock format, calculation method, juristic school, theme choice) are stored locally in your browser using localStorage.
  • Daily Verse History: Quran verses you've viewed are cached locally to enable navigation between verses.

2. Third-Party Services

We rely on the following external APIs to provide core functionality. When you use our extension, these services receive standard HTTP request data including your IP address, user agent, timestamp, and in some cases your coordinates:

  • Aladhan API (api.aladhan.com) – Provides Islamic prayer times and Hijri date conversion. Receives: your latitude, longitude, calculation method preferences, and request metadata (IP, user agent).
  • Alquran Cloud API (api.alquran.cloud) – Supplies Quranic Arabic text and English translations. Receives: ayah number and request metadata (IP, user agent). No personal data is transmitted.
  • BigDataCloud (api.bigdatacloud.net) – Reverse geocoding to convert coordinates into a readable city/country name. Receives: your latitude and longitude only.
  • OpenStreetMap Nominatim (nominatim.openstreetmap.org) – Location search for manual city selection. Receives: your search query and request metadata (IP, user agent).
  • SupportKori (www.supportkori.com) – Optional support widget. Only loaded when you explicitly click the support button. Their privacy policy governs their data practices.

3. Limited Use of Google API Data

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Any Google API data (if used) is solely for providing or improving the extension's core functionality and is not transferred to third parties for advertising, personalization, or credit-worthiness purposes.

4. Data Storage & Retention

All personal data remains in your browser. Nothing is sent to our own servers. Data persists until you clear browser storage or uninstall the extension.

You can reset all stored data by opening Settings → Reset Defaults or by clearing your browser's site data for this extension.

5. Your Rights

You have full control:

  • Disable location access at any time via browser settings. The extension will fall back to Mecca as the default location.
  • Clear all stored preferences and history from Settings.
  • Uninstall the extension at any time – no residual data transmission occurs.
  • No account creation or personally identifiable information is required.
