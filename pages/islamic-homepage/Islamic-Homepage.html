Features

Everything you need,
spiritually designed.

Built for busy Muslims who want to stay connected to their Salah without interrupting their workflow.

🕌
01

Accurate Prayer Times

GPS-based with 20+ regional methods (Muslim World League, ISNA, Umm Al-Qura, Diyanet). Includes Hanafi/Shafi Asr, high-latitude adjustments, two midnight modes (Standard/Jafari), real-time countdown, color-coded progress bars. Visual indicators for forbidden times (Sunrise, Zawal, Ghurub).

GPS 20+ Methods Real-time Forbidden Times
📖
02

Daily Quran Verse

A fresh Ayah every day with Arabic text and English translation. Navigate verse history and jump directly to Quran.com for full context. Works offline with local caching.

Arabic + English Offline Ready
🌌
03

Cinematic Backgrounds

Real-time gradients transitioning through five distinct prayer-phase palettes. Night sky mode with procedurally generated stars, shooting stars, and God rays sweeping the screen at dawn. GPU-optimised, zero battery drain.

Dynamic GPU-Optimised
🗺️
04

Smart Location

One-time geolocation permission — never tracked in background. Auto-detects your country and suggests the appropriate prayer calculation method. Global city database for manual search. Falls back to Makkah if denied.

Private Global Cities
⚙️
05

Deep Customisation

12/24h clock, calculation method, juristic school, theme selector (Default Islamic Night), high-latitude mode, midnight mode. A settings modal with live preview before applying ensures nothing is a surprise. Reset to factory defaults anytime.

Live Preview Reset Anytime
🔕
06

Respectful Notifications

Optional prayer time alerts with beautiful modal popups. 5-minute pre-prayer reminder, alarm sound with 30-second auto-stop. Cross-tab coordination ensures only one alert shows at a time. Disabled by default to respect your focus.

Respectful Cross-Tab Auto-Stop
☕
07

Support This Project

Optional "Buy me a coffee" support button that appears periodically. Fully dismissible and can be toggled from the About modal. Your support helps maintain this independent extension.

Optional Independent Dev Dismissible
